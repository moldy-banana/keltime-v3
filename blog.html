Today I said something super embarrassing that I shouldn't have said. I didn't even realise the implications of it till I said it. I told one of the best high school horn players in my district that I was going to accept being a mediocre horn player. I told him that I believed that at some point, some of the talent had to be innate.

THAT. WAS. SO. FUCKING. STUPID!!! Not only did I totally ignore the probably countless hours that the actually good people spend practicing, IT WAS SO BAD AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHh I'm currently reevaluating my position on life and I want to go hide underground for the rest of my life.

The truth is that I have been knowingly and unknowingly digging myself into a hole for years. I had slowly started to believe that in the end I wouldn't ever add up to anything because I wasn't born "smart" or "quick-minded" like all the other prodigies I saw around me.

I had truly started to believe in the ridiculous notion that talent was fixed. That I couldn't get any better at playing the horn. That I would never, ever, ever for the rest of my life, amount to anything at all. And I told myself repeatedly the same mantra. And then I said it in front of someone else, for the first time.

It's been a long time coming. This was a path that I should have noticed -that I probably did notice- but still let myself go down. Self destruction, am I right? So I wrote myself a little sticky note. The things below are important lines that I hope to never forget for the rest of my life:

  • Intelligence and talent can grow.
  • I can learn anything I want.
  • My EFFORT and ATTITUDE determine my abilities.
  • Skills are BUILT, not born.
  • I can improve with effort and practice.

It feels kind of corny. I feel like a cornball. Whatever, I guess it's time to go practice the horn now...
