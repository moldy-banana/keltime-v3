About the band

Yorushika is a band formed by composer n-buna and vocalist suis. Since 2017, they have released 5 studio albums and 3 extended plays. The name Yorushika is taken from a lyric in their song "The Clouds and the Ghost": 夜しかもう眠れずに (yoru shika mo nemurezu ni) - "Now without being able to sleep except at night".

Albums

Themes

Yorushika's lyrics and song titles draw from works of literature, including those of Ozaki Hosai and George Orwell. For example, Thoughtcrime is a song in the album Plagiarism, while also being a term used in Orwell's book 1984. The song title Algernon is a reference to the book Flowers for Algernon by David Keyes.

My Favorite Album + Songs

Plagiarism (2020) is definitely my favorite album from Yorushika. This album is a concept album; one that tells a story of "a man who plagiarizes music". Not only are the songs bangers, there is also a novel that expands upon the album written by n-buna himself. You can find the translation on reddit.

One of the best songs (and my favorite song) on the album is Flower and Badger Game. It's sweet, melancholic, and upbeat all in one. The lyrics expand on the thoughts of the plagiarist in the album, which expands on the lore of the plagiarist in the album. Crazy lore btw. Highly recommend!!

Shura

Shura has such an upbeat instrumental that makes you want to dance. It makes you forget the meaning of the word Shura.

According to Quora (sob), the term is used to describe a person who fell into a situation where they must fight an endless war against something. Anyways this song is a banger and I lowk relate??

Madder

Madder is my lazy day anthem. Hands down one of the best songs I've heard in my life, ever. I feel so calm and at peace listening to this song. The instruments and suis are amazing!! To note, the erhu has a wonderful soul-transcending solo.

The music videos are quite stunning, so I totally recommend watching those as part of the Yorushika experience.
