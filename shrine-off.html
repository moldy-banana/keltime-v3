OFF (2008) is an RPG developed by Mortis Ghost and Alias Conrad Coldwood... In which we follow the protagonist, "The Batter", on a sacred mission to purify the world- whatever that means.

The only reason I found out about this game was because it was on a random proxy site ifykyk. The three bold letters stood out against a background of noise. OFF. Very cool game.

The gameplay was interesting. The puzzles
were fun. Battle system gave me anxiety
because it was in real time.

Music: 9/10 (atmospheric!!)
Story: 10/10 (soo good)
Gameplay: 8/10 (iconic)
