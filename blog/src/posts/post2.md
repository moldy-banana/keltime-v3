Since the Fall rowing season has ended, I have had an extra 3 hours in my day to do random shit. 
This includes a non-exhaustive list of... watching youtube videos, reading 20+ hours worth of books (cough cough: the call of the wind), and scrolling through the undiscovered lands of tumblr. 

Although this all sounds very unproductive,  recording these activities will definitely for sure make the past time I have wasted sound less... unproductive.

**Youtube links will have tracking removed, so don't worry! :D**

### [Crystal Claire (Youtube Series)](https://youtu.be/XPG1Ak5rpFY){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}
**Content Warning: Blood, Horror (a little), Disturbing Imagery, Death.**
Another common win from one of my favorite producers. Product, the creator of *Dirty Donna*, *Dirty Snow*, and *Mother*, has recently finished their latest series; *Crystal Claire*. And honestly? It's amazing. 
Back with their signature animation style (one of the most unique ones I've ever seen), Product manages 
to disturb me in the best way possible. If you have a good 50 minutes to spare, I would recommend watching 
*Crystal Claire*.



### [Standing Still in a Moving Scene (Album)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yNFWVRiYuPE){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}
I came into this album by the artist Hyakkei (百景) expecting nothing but was pleasantly surprised to find some banger math rock to listen to while studying. My favorite track has to be the first one: *Kagefumi*.
I thought I discovered something pretty niche... (not). Apparently this album went viral on TikTok? Oh well. 
Math rock is fucking good so listen to this album ^^


### Wind Breaker (Manga)
I was recommended this manga from C at Robotics last year. It was surprisingly good, but I stopped reading it after I exhausted all of the chapters. I binged some of the newest chapters this week. Also recently 
found out that *Absolute Zero* by natori was the opening song for the anime?? One of the most unique 
things about *Wind Breaker* is the lack of flat characters. They all get their spotlight to shine, making for a lovable and unique cast- It's hard to choose a favorite.

### Endacopia (Video Game)
You know it. You love it. You're too poor so you watched Markiplier play it. It's *Endacopia*!! Taking "a look inside", it's about a boy named Mellow who explores a bizarre and disturbing world. It's exactly this 
kind of world that draws people in, especially me. The clown couple is the cutest honestly... 
![the two clowns, Ticketa and Ticketo sharing a sandwich. How cute!](/_site/assets/image.png)

### [Racing Mount Pleasant (Live Performance)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G8ADRLWMvAY){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}
I don't know how I didn't discover *Racing Mount Pleasant* before, but this band is genuinely 
a master at creating the highest of highs someone can experience while listening to music. 
This band is unique, as there are trumpet and saxophone players!? Adding band instruments 
to any song almost instantly elevates it... The rolling rhythms of *Racing Mount Pleasant* make every 
song seem like they have somewhere to go, somewhere to be, without sounding rushed. My favorite song is
their self-titled song from their self-titled album: *Racing Mount Pleasant*. How many times have I said "Racing Mount Pleasant"..? Go take a listen!!
