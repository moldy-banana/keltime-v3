Slowly falling into autumn's grasp, the warmth of the world begins to lapse...

It's getting colder

I went to homecoming the other day. Usually it's the before parties that are the best part of homecoming. The actual dance is quite boring. Somehow, the music was worse than last year. Over 80% of it wasn't hype enough to dance to. I went to dinner at a Japanese/Korean restaurant, though they specialised in sushi and ramen.

Now that part was really fun. My friends all ordered different things. I got the vegetarian sushi combo even though I wasn't vegetarian, but the sweet potato sushi sounded really interesting to me. Also it was only $20 (on the cheaper side). Getting the vegetarian sushi combo was probably the best decision I have ever made because I absolutely don't regret it.

The whole combo had a vegetable roll, an avocado roll, and a sweet potato tempura roll. The sweet potato roll was definitely the highlight. One bite and I was hooked; it was the perfect amount of sweet and slighty salty and it tasted good even when dipped in soy sauce. I tried to pace myself by finishing the vegetable and avocado rolls first, but all I could think about was the sweet potato.

The meal was so good and filling I was surprised. I didn't satisfy my fish craving but the vegetarian combo was good enough. I bought a matcha latte afterwards and it was lowk bad so I think I'm gonna stick with mango ice tea drinks for the foreseeable future.

12/9/2026

What someone carries around says a lot about them. Some carry calipers everywhere. Some carry a sixty-four ounce plastic water bottle everywhere.

The majority of each week, I carry my North Face Recon with me everywhere. Inside my backpack I carry 3 notebooks. Two are KOKUYO Campus B5 80 sheet notebooks and one is this random 5 subject ringed notebook that I have yet to find a use for. I love how the Campus notebooks are smooth and can lay completely flat. I never noticed how much that mattered before but I feel like note-taking has been much better because the notebook doesn't get in the way.

Note-taking wise, I carry all my stationery in a red MUJI nylon pen case. it looks like this...

To give you more insight, I am a high school student taking classes such as Spanish 3, AP US History, AP Chemistry, and AP Calculus BC. Some of my classes require handwritten notes and homework.

I take most of my notes with the uni kuru toga mechanical pencil in 0.5mm 2B lead. HB lead is difficult to write with. I don't know how anyone can use that and not want to use something softer. I also have a uni jetstream 0.5mm ballpoint pen inside. I used to use it a lot, but I ran out of correction tape.

I use the Tombow MONO eraser. I haven't found a better eraser yet because the MONO can erase cleanly. I have no complaints. I recommend buying a black eraser because it won't look dirty after erasing. It may not seem like it, but using a black eraser over a white one makes me feel significantly better.

I also carry around a red gel pen for corrections and some ZEBRA mildliner highlighters. They may not be the best at highlighting graphite, gel, or water based inks but the colors make me happy.

My planner was an impulse buy from MUJI, but it's also really cute so I'm not complaining. It's a pocket-sized September start monthly and weekly calendar with a white cover. It gets dirty easily, but there are pockets on the inside to store little papers in. I decorated the inside cover with animal stickers. I really like aquatic animals.

Although it makes my pencil case fatter, I also stuff my dot roll tape and earbuds case inside too. I find that having tape is very handy, even though I barely use it (other than for scrapbooking).

Outside of that, you can always find me with gum, water, and snacks. That's basically it. I carry around some version of a macbook for school, but I use a Lenovo 16 inch laptop outside of that. It probably weighs 10 pounds and it looks like a brick. Would make a good weapon 10/10 would recommend. The fan is always running high too, but that may be because I have 50+ tabs open at all times + vscode.
