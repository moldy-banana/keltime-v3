Note-taking wise, I carry all my stationery in a red MUJI nylon pen case. it looks like this...

To give you more insight, I am a high school student taking classes such as Spanish 3, AP US History, AP Chemistry, and AP Calculus BC. Some of my classes require handwritten notes and homework.

I take most of my notes with the uni kuru toga mechanical pencil in 0.5mm 2B lead. HB lead is difficult to write with. I don't know how anyone can use that and not want to use something softer. I also have a uni jetstream 0.5mm ballpoint pen inside. I used to use it a lot, but I ran out of correction tape.

I use the Tombow MONO eraser. I haven't found a better eraser yet because the MONO can erase cleanly. I have no complaints. I recommend buying a black eraser because it won't look dirty after erasing. It may not seem like it, but using a black eraser over a white one makes me feel significantly better.

I also carry around a red gel pen for corrections and some ZEBRA mildliner highlighters. They may not be the best at highlighting graphite, gel, or water based inks but the colors make me happy.

My planner was an impulse buy from MUJI, but it's also really cute so I'm not complaining. It's a pocket-sized September start monthly and weekly calendar with a white cover. It gets dirty easily, but there are pockets on the inside to store little papers in. I decorated the inside cover with animal stickers. I really like aquatic animals.

Although it makes my pencil case fatter, I also stuff my dot roll tape and earbuds case inside too. I find that having tape is very handy, even though I barely use it (other than for scrapbooking).

Outside of that, you can always find me with gum, water, and snacks. That's basically it. I carry around some version of a macbook for school, but I use a Lenovo 16 inch laptop outside of that. saturday the 13th of january

in my dream i was on the beach, walking towards the waves. my older sister dipped her legs into the water first, and i followed, stepping deeper and deeper as the water rose against my legs.

i woke up in my bed, pulling the quilt off my body to discover large, slowly moving bumps moving up across the skin of my legs. just then, i was transported. a doctor's gloved hand gently pinched a bulging bump of skin on a patient's arm that wasn't mine. "now, make sure it doesn't move." the doctor said, slowly pinching the area harder. something squirmed underneath the skin. with an unceremonious squelch, a writhing worm was forced out of the skin. pale and flesh colored, there was nothing it could do except slide off the patient's forearm and fall to the floor as blood oozed from the new wound.

i knew what i had to do. with each passing second, the bumps moved further up, closer to my heart. i leaned forward. with each new stream of blood dripping and splattering onto the mattress, i isolated each bump between my forefinger and thumb. each time, a writhing, flesh colored worm came out. some were short and fat, others just longer than my pinky finger and as thin as a noodle. the great mass of blood-sucking worms escaped hastily, leaving nothing but trails of blood in their wake.

when i woke up for real in the morning i was checking my legs and bed to see if there were worms lol

"I'm doing alright, lowk tweaking out bc of school tho." I responded. He sighed and replied with a knowing look: "Yeah, I definitely get that. Well, it's time to lock in now." He still looked tired. The same bags under his tired eyes. The same cold, empty room.

unknown date, fall of 25

I was sitting in a classroom and the professor at the front was lecturing. on the table was a plastic cup filled with a purple, viscous liquid. I knew it to be poison. "Now, will everyone take a sip from their cups?" The teacher instructed, and I took a sip of the cold, purple liquid that had no flavor. I closed my eyes, the viscous stuff slid down my throat. I could hear my heartbeat picking up, thudding in my chest. "And to all those that don't want to die, and haven't taken a sip from their cups, please take a small sip," The professor requested. At this moment I regretted drinking so much from the cup, though it was only a sip.

Warning: downer post below. it's kind of stressful.

ooo a sandwich

Somewhere in my head, I just want to give up. In life, in trying. This kind of stuff should have already been prevented after reading so many articles about whimsy, the joy of living, of working hard, and of finding success after failure.

But I still feel this way, deep down. I wonder if anyone has noticed it yet. When someone asks me, "What is your goal?" "Where will you go?" "What are you going to achieve this year?" "What did you achieve today?" I don't have an answer. My throat dries up and the words fly away, because those words never existed in the first place. Whatever ends up coming out of my mouth is weak, shaky, and unsure. Something made up in the moment just to escape furthur questioning.

It feels like there's some sort of mental warfare that I can't win. I know the consequences of giving up. I don't find joy in a boring life. I don't want to go day-by-day on meager scraps of whatever I can get. Even so, I don't want to work for that challenging, fulfilling life. I find myself giving up. There is a wanting to settle for less, to accept that I may never do anything, be anything remarkable at all.

I think I was always this kind of person. When I was younger, I would microwave my food for a minute, sometimes a minute thirty and call it a day. It didn't seem so bad, settling for something half lukewarm instead of a piping hot meal. At least I didn't have to wait for it to cool. It was already cold. It's easier that way.

Somewhere deep down, I don't even want to try. I don't think I'll ever tell anyone that, though. Why do I have to research for scholarships? Why do I have to plan my future? I know the answer. I know why. But the 'me' still echoes: "settle for less. watch youtube. do something other than studying and working out. other than building a future for yourself. Because it's easier that way."

People talk about delayed gratification. Working for the reward. "No pain no gain", right? I know, I know. I think I know. "Trying now will give me an easier future." I know. But sitting here at my desk surrounded by textbooks and stationery, listening to some minecraft music for studying on my computer, I can't help but want to drop everything. I want nothing.

Fortunately or unfortunately, that is not a choice. The problem must be that I do not have goals. It must be that I cannot see myself 5 years in the future. People achieve things because they have goals to achieve. I have measly wisps of dreams floating away as we speak. Otherwise known as: No Direction in Life.

Maybe the whole problem is personality. Other people out there are trying so hard. Building bridges, building towers to their sky-high goals. Outgoing, cunning, opportunistic, hard working. Writing essay after essay, research paper after research paper. Winning award after award. Yet I still sit here. There is nothing for me to show.

I've done nothing all day. Maybe I should eat. I must eat, or else I won't have energy to erg after this. It's not like I've done that well either. Maybe I just want to be a basement dweller. Living off of tap water and doritos. Even then I have a far away dream of living in a bustling city and going partying on friday nights after a day at work. Of course, this is not a be-all-end-all. Nothing is ever black or white. But I still can't help but think this way.

Time is running out. Did it already run out? Who knows.
